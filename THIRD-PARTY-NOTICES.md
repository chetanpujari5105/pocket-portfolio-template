## Packages

Installed by `npm ci` from `package-lock.json`: 810 packages in all. By licence: MIT 661,
Apache-2.0 45, ISC 33, BSD-2-Clause 17, MPL-2.0 14, BSD-3-Clause 10, LGPL-3.0-or-later 10 (plus 4
mixed with Apache-2.0/MIT), MIT-0 4, BlueOak-1.0.0 3, CC0-1.0 2, and one each of CC-BY-4.0,
(CC-BY-4.0 AND MIT), (MIT OR EUPL-1.1+), (MIT OR CC0-1.0), Python-2.0 and 0BSD. One package
(`webgl-constants`, via drei → detect-gpu) declares no licence in its package.json; its LICENSE file
is MIT. To see every package and its licence: `npx license-checker --summary` (or `--csv`).

Packages that are not plain permissive licences, and why that is fine here:

- **LGPL-3.0-or-later**: `@img/sharp-libvips-*` and `@img/sharp-*` (libvips, used by `sharp` to
  resize images at build time). Installed as prebuilt, unmodified binaries; nothing from them is
  sent to visitors.
- **MPL-2.0**: `lightningcss` (CSS minifier in the Tailwind build) and `axe-core` /
  `@axe-core/playwright` (accessibility checks in tests). Development tools, used unmodified.
- **CC-BY-4.0**: `caniuse-lite` (browser support data used by the build tools).
- **(CC-BY-4.0 AND MIT)**: `@fortawesome/free-brands-svg-icons`: only the LinkedIn mark is copied
  into the site, credited in Settings.
